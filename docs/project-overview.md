# Project Overview

## Introduction
This project explores teamwork and leadership and their role in achieving organizational and personal goals.

## Importance of Teamwork
Teamwork encourages cooperation, trust, shared responsibility, and better results.

## Importance of Leadership
Leadership helps guide, motivate, and support team members towards a common goal.

## Learning Outcomes
- Better communication
- Coordination and cooperation
- Leadership development
- Decision-making skills
- Conflict management
